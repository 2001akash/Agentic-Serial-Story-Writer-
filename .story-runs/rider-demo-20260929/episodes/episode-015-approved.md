# Episode 15: Deadly Delivery

Alex’s final delivery for the day is a small, unmarked package addressed to a number on the seventeenth floor of Building 17. He hesitates at the stairwell, the whisper from the hallway still echoing his name. The air is thick with the scent of old wood and something metallic—like the cold, dampness that clung to the walls after the 1923 fire.

He slides the parcel into the door, the brass plaque on the latch catching the light. A faint, translucent figure flickers in the corner of his eye, a shape that looks like a man in a 1920s uniform, his face blurred. The figure leans close and whispers, “Bring them peace, and you’ll live.” The voice is not male or female, but a hiss of wind through a cracked window.

Alex’s heart pounds. He pulls the package out, holding it like a talisman. The whisper grows louder, a chorus of names—Samuel Ortiz, Maya Patel, Luis Reyes—tossed into the wind. He turns the package over and sees a faint, inverted‑triangle symbol etched into the cardboard, matching the one on the seventh‑floor mailbox.

He steps into the dim stairwell, the echo of his footsteps bouncing off the stone. The corridor ahead is lined with doors, each marked with a number and a name that matches the 2023 death register. He pulls a breath, then stops at the seventeenth‑floor door. The door is older than the rest, its hinges creaking as if they’ve never been opened in years.

The whisper turns into a hiss of wind that seems to come from inside the door. Alex hesitates, then pushes. The door slides open on a rusted hinge, revealing a narrow, dimly lit hallway that leads to a low, charred room. The air is stale, the smell of old smoke thick.

He steps into the room and the whisper stops. The room is small, with a single table that bears a brittle, folded plan of Building 17. The plan shows seventeen narrow doorways, each labeled with a name. The names line up perfectly with the 2023 death register. The plan also shows a hidden compartment beneath the lobby, marked with a question mark.

Alex’s hand trembles as he lifts the plan. The paper feels brittle, as if it’s been handled for decades. He sees a faint, hand‑written note in Maya’s script: "Witness—Maya Patel". The note is torn, as if someone had tried to remove it.

A sudden knock rattles the wall behind the table. Alex turns, heart racing. The knock is faint, but he can hear it coming from the sealed wall that leads to the low room.

He reaches out, feeling the cold, damp stone. The whisper returns, a low, mournful hum. Alex knows he’s at a threshold—he’s standing between the living and the dead, between the curse and the chance to break it.

He pulls the plan close, the whisper still audible, and the final question lingers in the air: Can Alex survive the encounter?
